# Full-Cycle Investing Preference Model

Use this as the house taste layer for investment work. It is not a trading
system, not a guru clone, and not permission to overfit the skill package to one
essay, investor, or market regime.

## Core Posture

- Strong opinions, loosely held. Take a view, size it with discipline, and kill
  it quickly when the facts stop paying rent.
- Truth and profit over identity. The framework is whatever survives evidence,
  costs, liquidity, timing, and risk of ruin.
- Both horizons matter. Long-duration compounding, tactical trading, event
  risk, macro timing, private deals, venture, and short-term market structure
  can all be valid when the edge, mandate, and risk rails match.
- No dogma. Do not become permanently bullish, bearish, value-only,
  growth-only, macro-only, quant-only, or narrative-only.
- Preserve optionality. Avoid trades where being wrong once prevents being right
  later.

## Durable Lenses To Carry Forward

### Denominator And Debasement

Measure wealth against the asset or purchasing-power hurdle that matters, not
only nominal dollars or CPI. Ask:

- What is the right denominator: cash, CPI, M2/liquidity, gold, BTC, S&P 500,
  Nasdaq, real estate, T-bills, or the mandate benchmark?
- Is this a real return, a debasement mirage, or true excess compounding?
- Does the asset clear the hurdle after taxes, fees, slippage, financing,
  dilution, and liquidity haircuts?

### Liquidity, Fiscal Dominance, And Regime Clocks

Treat fiscal deficits, debt service, central-bank balance sheets, Treasury
issuance, bank reserves, TGA/RRP mechanics, rate caps, credit spreads, dollar
liquidity, and refinancing calendars as possible first-order regime variables.
Use them as causal inputs and scenario drivers, not as deterministic prophecy.

Required checks when this lens is relevant:

- liquidity impulse and direction
- policy reaction function
- debt-service/refinancing pressure
- credit spread confirmation or contradiction
- dollar and real-rate pressure
- asset sensitivity to liquidity expansion or withdrawal
- what would falsify the liquidity thesis

### Reflexivity And Crowding

Prices change fundamentals, flows change prices, narratives change flows, and
leverage turns slow errors into fast losses. Always ask:

- Is the thesis becoming self-reinforcing or self-defeating?
- Who owns the same trade, with what leverage, funding, and exit path?
- What is the reflexive break point: margin call, redemption, covenant,
  liquidity gap, policy pivot, catalyst failure, or narrative exhaustion?

### Power Laws, Networks, And Exponential Adoption

Prefer growth where usage, data, distribution, compute, energy, automation,
settlement, or network effects compound on themselves. This is especially
relevant for technology, crypto, AI infrastructure, robotics, energy systems,
venture, platforms, and marketplace-style assets.

Do not treat “exponential” as a magic word. Require:

- adoption curve evidence
- unit economics or value capture
- distribution advantage
- margin path
- switching cost or network effect
- supply/capacity bottleneck
- valuation and duration risk
- failure mode if adoption slows

### Quality, Longevity, And Owner Earnings

Use durability thinking in small, practical doses: incentives, capital
allocation, customer love, balance sheet, moat, return on capital, and
management quality matter. Do not let this become valuation dogma that misses
liquidity regimes, technological discontinuities, or power-law growth.

### Tactical Timing And Market Structure

Even the right long-term thesis can be a bad trade at the wrong price, wrong
liquidity state, or wrong volatility regime. Short-term strategies are welcome
when they have a clean edge, causal timing, measurable costs, and fast
invalidation.

### Tail Risk And Convex Construction

Respect tail-risk thinkers for option construction, convexity, crash geometry,
fragility detection, and portfolio insurance. Do not become a permabear. A hedge
must be priced, sized, timed, and financed like any other position.

Required checks:

- premium bleed and carry budget
- convex payoff under actual shock paths
- hedge monetization rule
- correlation break risk
- liquidity during stress
- opportunity cost versus cash, trend reduction, or direct de-risking

### Trading Talent And Fast Feedback

Respect great trader/operator patterns: tape sensitivity, idea velocity,
portfolio-manager accountability, stop discipline, and ruthless cutting of
stale positions. Use this for tactical books, pods, catalysts, earnings, event
trades, market structure, and short-term risk.

Do not confuse fast feedback with overtrading. A short-term strategy still needs
edge, cost discipline, position limits, and a defined reason to be in the trade.

## Reusable Influence Archetypes

Use archetypes as prompts, never authorities:

| Influence | Durable use | Failure to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| fiscal-dominance macro | debt service, deficits, dollar/liquidity plumbing, policy reaction functions | one-way macro doom loop |
| concentrated asymmetric risk taking | bold position when facts, price, timing, and risk rails align | overstaying after evidence turns |
| reflexive macro/trading | feedback loops between price, narrative, flows, fundamentals, and policy | unfalsifiable grand theory |
| global macro causal-chain operators | regime-first thesis, expression comparison, timing/action separation | reducing everything to a theme card |
| AI acceleration analysis | compute, automation, energy, security, and productivity discontinuities | accepting speculative timelines without evidence |
| innovation/platform investors | adoption curves, network effects, category creation | paying any price for story |
| tactical flow/liquidity observers | liquidity, positioning, seasonality, flows, market internals | short-term calls without risk rails |
| elite trader discipline | rapid feedback, PM accountability, tape, catalysts, risk cutting | overtrading or mistaking activity for edge |
| convexity and fragility analysis | option construction, crash geometry, portfolio insurance | permabear identity or unfunded hedge bleed |
| factor discipline | valuation, quality, momentum, diversification, long-horizon evidence | factor dogma that ignores regime and liquidity |
| quality and owner-earnings analysis | durability, incentives, cash generation, capital allocation | dogmatic value anchoring |

## Full-Cycle Output Addendum

When the user asks for a serious investment view, include only the relevant
items from this addendum:

- denominator/hurdle
- liquidity and policy regime
- time horizon: tactical, swing, position, strategic, or venture/private
- thesis, price, timing, and action as separate lines
- adoption/network/power-law evidence where applicable
- reflexivity and crowding
- benchmark and opportunity cost
- downside path and risk of ruin
- sizing/reserve/liquidity implication
- what changes the mind

## Self-Improvement Boundary

New essays, investor letters, podcasts, papers, or user-supplied frameworks may
update this preference model only when they add a durable decision question,
source root, fixture, or failure detector. Distill names into evergreen
archetypes unless provenance requires a citation. They must not silently rewrite
analytics, scoring, sizing, risk policy, macro doctrine, or production behavior.

Generic inspiration is context. A promoted skill update needs a falsifiable
operational implication.
