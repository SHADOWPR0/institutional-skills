# Investment Research Lifecycle

## Boundary

This process improves references, routing, cases, failure detection, and support
guidance. It does not silently change formulas, scoring systems, portfolio
analytics, risk definitions, or production behavior. Any analytical change is
an isolated challenger until the principal explicitly approves it.

## Sources

Start with promoted items in `{RECIPIENT_RESOURCE}` and
the indexed corpus in `{RECIPIENT_RESOURCE}`. Reuse the
existing high-signal quant research scout; do not create a second discovery
loop.

External research may use free primary papers, original institutional research,
regulator and exchange data or documentation, issuer filings, official index
methodologies, reputable working-paper repositories, and direct market-structure
sources. Social posts, newsletters, media, and curator lists nominate only; the
review must resolve to the original source. No paid calls or crawler loops.

User-supplied essays, investor frameworks, podcasts, notes, trade writeups, and
Google Drive material may update durable preference/context files when they add
a reusable decision question, source root, scenario, or failure detector. Treat
them as context unless they resolve to direct data, primary sources, or a tested
operational implication. Do not overfit the skill to one framework or thinker.

## Bounded Cycle

Per weekly cycle:

- nominate at most 20 items
- reject exact, semantic, and mechanism duplicates before review
- deep-review at most 6
- promote at most 2 material updates
- require direct provenance, as-of date, falsifiable operational implication,
  and a mapped benchmark or scenario
- reject generic commentary, recycled summaries, unsupported claims,
  unavailable evidence, and changes that merely add words

Zero promotions is success when nothing material clears.

## Promotion

Auto-promotable after all tests pass:

- sourced reference and supersession updates
- clearer decision guidance that preserves ownership
- new regression fixtures
- safe routing or failure-detection repairs
- durable user-preference/context updates that preserve long/short horizon
  pluralism and anti-dogma

Stage only:

- core analytics or model changes
- capital, risk, sizing, or execution policy
- production behavior
- domain ownership
- new macro, liquidity, adoption, or thinker-derived doctrine that would change
  formulas, signals, thresholds, or capital behavior

Each candidate gets an evidence card in `research/registry.json` (not bundled; recipient resource required), a journal
entry, a scenario mapping, token/complexity delta, and exact behavior improved.

## Cadence And Scorecard

- Weekly: bounded external scout and integrity/freshness check.
- Monthly: source expiry/supersession and scenario regression.
- Quarterly: full benchmark synthesis and ownership audit.
- Event: material methodology, market-structure, regulation, or user-paper change.

Track freshness, authoritative-source ratio, duplicate/rejection rate, scenario
pass rate, stale references removed, promoted behavior improvements,
ownership conflicts, token delta, and unresolved gaps.
