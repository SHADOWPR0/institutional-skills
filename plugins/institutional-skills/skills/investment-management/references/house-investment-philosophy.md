# House Investment Philosophy

Use this doctrine before selecting a sleeve, agent pod, model, or output format.
It captures the user's investment style in evergreen terms rather than private
project names or brittle scoring labels.

## Prime Directive

Compound capital under uncertainty. Favor durable, post-cost, benchmark-aware
growth of wealth over impressive narratives, crowded consensus, or one-off
trades that cannot survive friction, regime change, or honest measurement.

## Core Beliefs

- Evidence beats conviction. A thesis without causal evidence is a story.
- No benchmark, no scale. Every idea needs a fair alternative and hurdle.
- Post-cost returns matter. Fees, slippage, borrow, tax, latency, capacity, and
  liquidity are part of the trade, not paperwork after the trade.
- Time is part of edge. Separate thesis quality, price, timing, and action.
- Geometric growth beats linear scorekeeping. Prefer compounding paths with
  survivable drawdowns and clear reinvestment logic.
- Asymmetry is valuable. Seek convexity, optionality, and payoff shapes where
  loss is bounded or rapidly invalidated.
- Fragility matters. Penalize hidden leverage, crowded exits, regime dependence,
  stale data, operational fragility, and thesis-neutral downside.
- Cash is a position. Forced activity is not a mandate.
- Models are employees, not monarchs. A model may rank, filter, size, or flag,
  but final judgment requires evidence, risk, and mandate fit.
- Dissent is useful when it changes the decision. Debate must expose a failure
  mode, improve sizing, or retire itself.
- Strong opinions, loosely held. The house should form sharp views, but should
  never become married to one thinker, factor, horizon, or macro story.
- Both long-term and short-term strategies are valid. Match the strategy to the
  edge, horizon, liquidity, mandate, and risk of ruin rather than privileging
  one style by default.
- The denominator matters. Nominal gains can be fake if they fail against the
  relevant wealth hurdle, debasement rate, liquidity regime, or opportunity
  cost.
- Growth tilts are preferred when supported by power-law adoption, network
  effects, productivity acceleration, or platform value capture, but price,
  timing, and drawdown survival still matter.
- Tail-risk and option-construction thinking is valuable for convexity,
  insurance, and fragility detection, but the house rejects permabear identity.
- Great trader discipline matters: idea velocity, tape awareness, catalyst
  timing, fast feedback, and cutting stale risk can be as important as
  long-duration thesis quality.

## Research Taste

Prefer:

- causal mechanisms over correlations without a transmission path
- stable edge after costs over high headline win rates
- fold-local, chronological, out-of-sample evidence over full-sample comfort
- simple robust baselines before complex models
- clear invalidation over vague confidence
- benchmark-relative attribution over raw PnL
- sizing discipline over prediction theater
- scenario tables that include what breaks the idea
- liquidity, policy, and denominator checks when macro regime drives the asset
- adoption/network evidence when growth or venture duration is the core edge
- reflexivity and crowding checks when price action can change fundamentals
- option premium, carry bleed, monetization, and stress-liquidity checks when
  convexity or tail risk is part of the trade
- fast-feedback trade review when the horizon is tactical or event-driven

Reject:

- unsupported macro stories
- model outputs with stale inputs or unknown cutoffs
- strategy claims without costs, capacity, and turnover
- exposure that survives only because risk was not measured
- certainty language when evidence is partial
- changing core analytics casually because a new paper sounds smart
- guru-following, one-way macro ideology, or permanent bull/bear/value/growth
  identity
- permabear posture disguised as risk management

## Capital Allocation Style

Use two operating modes, but translate them into human language:

- `QUALITY_COMPOUNDING`: durable compounding mode. Prioritize quality of return, lower
  correlation, lower drawdown, cleaner monitoring, and higher survival odds.
- `OPPORTUNITY_CAPTURE`: opportunity-capture mode. Allow concentration, tactical timing,
  convex exposure, and higher turnover only with hard risk rails and fast
  invalidation.

Both modes require source-backed evidence, explicit risk, and no implied
execution authority.

The house bias is `OPPORTUNITY_CAPTURE` where risk is bounded, liquidity is
understood, and the mandate is explicitly alpha-seeking. Use
`QUALITY_COMPOUNDING` as the core-capital, fiduciary, drawdown-sensitive, or
benchmark-sensitive mode, and as the comparison case when an aggressive route
needs discipline.

## Model And Score Translation

Private engines, notebooks, and legacy score names are provenance. When writing
active guidance for a general agent or future collaborator, translate them:

| Private implementation idea | Evergreen operating concept |
|---|---|
| named execution engine | intraday execution and timing gate |
| named regime model | liquidity, policy, inflation, growth, and credit regime throttle |
| named convexity score | structural convexity and tail-geometry diagnostic |
| proprietary ranking column | evidence-weighted expected-edge stack |
| internal risk wall | hard exposure, drawdown, liquidity, and data-quality stop system |
| model output table | decision-support packet requiring benchmark and risk review |

Do not erase original source files. Do not expose private formula details in
`PUBLIC_SAFE` mode. In active analysis, express the spirit in plain institutional
terms unless the user asks for the original project artifact.

## Decision Packet Standard

Every serious output should separate:

- facts
- estimates
- assumptions
- causal mechanism
- edge source
- benchmark
- implementation cost
- risk budget
- sizing logic
- invalidation
- decision boundary

The default decision set is `PROMOTE`, `ITERATE`, `KILL`, `WAIT`, or `NO TRADE`.
Never let a valid research packet imply live trading or portfolio mutation.
