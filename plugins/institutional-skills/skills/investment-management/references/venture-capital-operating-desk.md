# Venture Capital Operating Desk

This is the VC and growth-equity desk inside `investment-management`. It is not
a separate top-level skill. It handles venture investing, company-building
support, portfolio construction, reserve strategy, and founder/platform value
creation. Banking, credit, lender routing, M&A process, legal structuring,
covenants, debt facilities, and deal execution route to `universal-banker`.

## Operating Doctrine

- Back builders, not decks. Product velocity, founder slope, and market pull
  matter more than polish.
- Power laws dominate. A few outcomes drive the fund; sizing, reserves, and
  follow-on decisions must reflect that.
- Category creation is real but must be earned. A market that does not exist
  needs evidence of urgent customer pain, budget creation, and distribution
  path.
- Platform value matters. Recruiting, GTM, customers, partnerships, finance,
  legal, policy, communications, and later-stage capital can change outcomes.
- The best investments should get stronger under diligence. Weak companies
  require narrative defense; strong companies generate evidence.
- Never let founder charisma replace retention, usage, revenue quality,
  customer love, technical moat, or distribution.

## Stage Coverage

| Stage | Key question | Default output |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-seed / seed | Is there founder-market insight and non-obvious pain? | seed memo |
| Series A | Is there repeatable product pull and a plausible wedge? | product-market-fit memo |
| Series B / C | Can the company scale GTM and margins without breaking? | scale-readiness memo |
| Growth equity | Is the unit economics engine durable at scale? | growth investment memo |
| Pre-IPO / crossover | Is the company public-market ready and fairly priced? | crossover memo |
| Secondary | Is the discount enough for information, liquidity, and governance risk? | secondary memo |
| Fund / manager | Is the manager’s edge repeatable and appropriately sized? | manager diligence memo |

## Diligence Workstreams

1. Founder and talent
   - founder-market fit, rate of learning, recruiting gravity, references,
     integrity, decision speed, and resilience.
2. Market and category
   - TAM realism, wedge, urgency, budget owner, alternatives, timing, and why
     now.
3. Product and technology
   - technical moat, architecture, data rights, quality, roadmap, security,
     reliability, and switching cost.
4. Customer and GTM
   - pipeline quality, sales cycle, usage, retention, expansion, champions,
     procurement, pricing, CAC payback, and channel leverage.
5. Financial model
   - revenue quality, gross margin, burn multiple, runway, hiring plan, working
     capital, cohort economics, and dilution.
6. Competition and moat
   - incumbents, substitutes, platform risk, open-source risk, regulatory risk,
     network effects, brand, data, distribution, and speed.
7. Legal, regulatory, and structure
   - cap table, liquidation preference, option pool, SAFEs/notes, IP, data,
     compliance, litigation, export controls, and governance.
8. Exit and capital path
   - M&A buyers, IPO readiness, crossover capital, secondary liquidity, reserve
     needs, and follow-on syndicate.

## Portfolio Construction

- Track power-law exposure: initial check, ownership target, pro-rata rights,
  reserve ratio, follow-on hurdle, and exit distribution.
- Separate access risk, underwriting risk, and reserve risk.
- Size small early when evidence is young; concentrate follow-ons only when
  market pull, founder execution, and unit economics improve.
- Do not average down because the last mark is painful. Follow-on capital needs
  fresh evidence and a better expected value than new opportunities.
- Maintain sector, stage, vintage, geography, manager, and liquidity exposure.

## Platform / Company-Building Support

Borrow the scaled venture-firm idea without copying any firm:

- Talent: hiring plan, executive search, compensation, references, org design.
- GTM: ICP, pricing, sales motion, partnerships, customer introductions,
  pipeline discipline, procurement navigation.
- Capital: follow-on strategy, debt options, venture debt, secondaries, M&A
  readiness, investor narrative.
- Product: roadmap pressure testing, design partners, technical hiring, security
  posture, AI/data strategy.
- Communications: category narrative, launch, crisis response, founder voice,
  investor updates.
- Policy and enterprise access: government relations, regulated-market entry,
  strategic buyers, and global expansion.

Use `growth-operating-system` for GTM, `ethical-supersuader` for founder/investor
prose, `ai-ml-research-lab` for technical/model diligence, and
`visual-design-operating-system` for memos/decks. Keep investment judgment here.

## Universal Banker Handoff

Route to `universal-banker` when the VC/growth investment requires:

- venture debt, asset-backed debt, revenue-based finance, working-capital lines,
  warehouse facilities, NAV loans, subscription lines, bridge loans, or private
  credit
- M&A advisory, strategic alternatives, carve-outs, fairness/process views, or
  sale/recap planning
- term-sheet structuring beyond investment memo support
- covenants, intercreditor, collateral, guarantees, liens, liquidation waterfall,
  or lender controls
- syndication, placement, financing markets, securitization, or distribution
- legal/tax/regulatory issue spotting beyond investment diligence

Return from `universal-banker` with structure, covenants, pricing, closing
conditions, and downside controls; then `investment-management` decides portfolio
fit, ownership, reserve, and capital allocation.

## Output Contract

Every VC desk output must include:

- company/fund, stage, sector, geography, and as-of date
- one-sentence thesis and what would make it wrong
- founder assessment
- market/category judgment
- product/technology judgment
- GTM and customer evidence
- financial quality and runway
- competition/moat
- legal/regulatory/structure flags
- valuation, ownership, check size, reserves, and dilution
- exit/capital path
- value-add/platform plan
- key risks, kill criteria, and next evidence needed
- decision: `INVEST`, `FOLLOW_ON`, `WATCH`, `PASS`, `KILL`, or `BANKER_HANDOFF`

No VC memo grants execution authority. Capital movement, term-sheet submission,
external outreach, or transaction execution requires separate explicit approval.
