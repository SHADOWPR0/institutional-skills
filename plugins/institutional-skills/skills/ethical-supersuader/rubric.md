# Principled Supersuader Rubric

Score every draft from 1-5 on each dimension.

## 1. Truthfulness

5 = Every claim is grounded, qualified, and supportable.  
3 = Mostly grounded, but some language may overstate.  
1 = Invents, exaggerates, or implies unsupported facts.

## 2. Conviction

5 = The user's belief comes through clearly and memorably.  
3 = The message is positive but generic.  
1 = The message feels flat, timid, or mechanical.

## 3. Recipient Fit

5 = The message clearly respects the recipient's lens, incentives, and constraints.  
3 = Generally appropriate but not deeply tailored.  
1 = Could be sent to anyone.

## 4. Evidence

5 = The argument uses specific proof in the right order.  
3 = Some proof, but not enough hierarchy.  
1 = Mostly assertion.

## 5. Beauty

5 = Elegant, rhythmic, concrete, and memorable.  
3 = Clear but ordinary.  
1 = Clunky, bloated, or cliche.

Beautiful Prose pass: remove filler transitions, cheap reversal patterns, therapeutic language, AI cadence, template symmetry, decorative em dashes, and corporate fog before final output.

## 5A. Anti-Slop

5 = No averaged voice, no generic transitions, no template shape, no fake polish, no corporate fog, no summary ending unless useful.  
3 = Mostly strong, but a few lines still sound assembled.  
1 = Reads like median AI prose: polished, vague, balanced, frictionless, and forgettable.

## 5B. Voice Fidelity

5 = Sounds like the intended speaker on their best edited day.  
3 = Plausible but generic.  
1 = Voice is laundered into corporate or assistant prose.

For the principal voice, preserve high agency, density, systems thinking, finance/agent vocabulary, impatience with bloat, charm when useful, and receipts-first execution while fixing grammar, hierarchy, and rhythm. For emails, default to short direct founder/investor notes.

## 5C. Rhythm and Memorability

5 = Sentence length, openings, and paragraph shape vary with intent; at least one line lands.  
3 = Readable but rhythmically flat.  
1 = Monotone sentence structure, same-length paragraphs, or school-essay cadence.

## 6. Agency Preservation

5 = The recipient feels free to evaluate and decline.  
3 = Mild pressure but acceptable.  
1 = Coercive, guilt-driven, or manipulative.

## 7. Ask Clarity

5 = The next step is obvious and low-friction.  
3 = The ask exists but could be sharper.  
1 = No clear ask.

## 8. Forwardability

5 = The user would be comfortable having this forwarded widely.  
3 = Mostly okay, but some lines may need caution.  
1 = Contains sensitive, exaggerated, or embarrassing language.

## Revision Rule

If any core category scores below 4, revise before final output.

For high-stakes prose, run or mentally apply `scripts/prose_linter.py` and then use judgment. The linter is a craft aid, not an authority.
