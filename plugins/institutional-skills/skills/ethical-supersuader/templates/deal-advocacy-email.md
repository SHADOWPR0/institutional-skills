# Deal Advocacy Email Template

Use when the user is going to bat for a deal, founder, company, or opportunity.

## Structure

Subject: {{subject_line}}

Hi {{recipient_name}},

I wanted to put this in writing because I think {{company_or_deal}} deserves a closer look.

The simple version is this: {{core_thesis}}.

I know {{recipient_constraint_or_likely_objection}}. That is the right lens. But what keeps standing out to me is {{main_reason_for_conviction}}.

A few things make this feel different:

- {{evidence_point_1}}
- {{evidence_point_2}}
- {{evidence_point_3}}

The risk, as I see it, is {{honest_risk}}. But my read is that {{risk_counterpoint}}.

The reason I am leaning in is not because this is obvious. It is because {{why_non_obvious_but_compelling}}.

Would you be open to {{clean_ask}}?

Best,  
{{user_name}}

## Notes

Keep the tone calm, credible, and high-conviction.  
Avoid hype.  
Make the recipient feel respected, not cornered.
