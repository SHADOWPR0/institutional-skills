Subject: {{company}} <> {{investor_name}}

What I like here is {{core_thesis}}. The market has {{market_dynamic}}, and {{company}} seems to have found {{wedge_or_insight}}.

A few quick reasons I think this is worth your time:

- {{reason_1}}
- {{reason_2}}
- {{reason_3}}

{{founder_name}} - meet {{investor_name}}, who has spent a lot of time around {{relevant_area}} and whose lens I think would be valuable here.

{{user_name}}
