# Humanizer Pattern Addendum - 2026-06-15

This reference folds useful anti-AI-slop patterns from public `humanizer` and `humanizer-zh` skill packages into the canonical `ethical-supersuader` skill. It is a pattern library, not a separate writing front door.

## Core Rule

Do not try to make text "human" by adding quirks. Make the thinking sharper, the evidence more concrete, the rhythm less templated, and the voice more situated.

## High-Signal AI Tells To Remove

- significance inflation: `important`, `critical`, `vital`, `transformative`, `game-changing` without evidence
- generic attribution: `many people`, `some experts`, `research suggests` without source or ownership
- promotional gloss: `seamless`, `robust`, `powerful`, `unlock`, `elevate`, `revolutionary`
- operator/corporate gloss: process language that sounds strategic while hiding the actual actor, decision, risk, number, or ask
- formulaic openings: `in today's landscape`, `at its core`, `in a world where`
- helper-meta prose: `let's explore`, `here are the key takeaways`, `this piece examines`
- false balance: `not only X but also Y`, `while X, it is also Y` when hierarchy matters
- synonym cycling: repeating the same idea with near-synonyms to create fake depth
- rule-of-three padding: neat triads that sound balanced but add no information
- tailing negations and reversal pivots: `not X, but Y` used as a reflex
- title-case microheadings in casual prose
- bolded sentence fragments used as fake structure
- em dash dependence, decorative semicolons, and punctuation as personality
- excessive hedging: `may`, `might`, `could`, `potentially`, `arguably` when uncertainty is not real
- sycophantic warmth: praise, validation, or enthusiasm that the relationship has not earned
- generic conclusion paragraphs that recap instead of landing

## Rewrite Moves

- Replace status adjectives with mechanisms, numbers, constraints, names, or consequences.
- Replace vague actors with the actual person, company, market, customer, or system.
- Replace a symmetrical list with the single strongest point, then the proof.
- Replace empty transitions with causality, contrast, or a paragraph break.
- Replace corporate operator nouns with the actual thing: person, decision, system, risk, ask, model, customer, market, file, or number.
- Keep warmth and charm where the relationship deserves it. Cut ceremony, not humanity.
- Preserve useful human edges: directness, specificity, tension, and judgment.
- Cut any sentence whose only job is to sound polished.
- Keep uncertainty only where it protects truth.

## the principal Voice Guardrail

For the principal, do not launder intensity into generic executive polish. Infer voice only from approved samples; retain appropriate warmth, useful detail, brevity and directness.

The edit should make him clearer and more elegant. It should not make him sound house-trained by a marketing department.

## Final Pass

Before returning high-stakes prose, check:

- Does every paragraph contain a claim, detail, decision, image, or ask?
- Can the reader tell who is acting and why?
- Did any sentence survive only because it sounds nice?
- Did the piece preserve agency without coercion?
- Would the principal recognize the taste, pressure, and point of view as his own?
