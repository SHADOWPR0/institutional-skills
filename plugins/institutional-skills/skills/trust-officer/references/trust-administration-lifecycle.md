# Trust Administration Lifecycle

## 1) Onboarding

- collect governing instrument, amendments, acceptance docs, tax IDs
- confirm trustee powers, situs, fiduciary standards
- open controls: approvals, account permissions, valuation cadence

## 2) Initial Funding and Transfer

- inventory incoming assets and liabilities
- validate title/registration and cost basis records
- establish principal/income accounting baseline

## 3) Steady-State Administration

- cash management, disbursements, fee processing
- principal/income allocations and reconciliation
- periodic reporting and beneficiary communications

## 4) Distribution Events

- apply distribution standard from governing terms
- perform liquidity and tax impact checks
- document rationale and approval chain

## 5) Tax and Year-End Cycle

- gather tax data, K-1/1099 dependencies, filing deadlines
- perform exception review and carryforwards tracking

## 6) Termination or Transition

- validate termination conditions
- final accounting and approval package
- close controls and archive records
