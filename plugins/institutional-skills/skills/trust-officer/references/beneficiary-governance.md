# Beneficiary Governance

## Communication Standards

- periodic transparent reporting
- documented responses to material inquiries
- plain-language explanations of decisions and constraints

## Conduct Standards

- consistent treatment aligned to governing terms
- de-escalation and mediation pathway for disputes
- privacy and confidentiality controls by role

## Escalation Ladder

1. case officer response
2. fiduciary committee review
3. legal/compliance escalation
4. formal dispute pathway
