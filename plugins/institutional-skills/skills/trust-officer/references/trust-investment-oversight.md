# Trust Investment Oversight

## Core Standards

- apply prudent investor discipline
- align portfolio with trust purpose and cash-flow needs
- respect governing concentration limits and restrictions
- rebalance with tax awareness and liquidity constraints

## Oversight Process

1. define policy benchmark and risk budget
2. review concentration, liquidity, and drawdown posture
3. monitor manager/sleeve performance and mandate adherence
4. document rebalancing and risk-control decisions

## Key Metrics

- policy tracking error
- concentration by issuer/asset class
- liquidity runway under stress
- drawdown vs approved limits
