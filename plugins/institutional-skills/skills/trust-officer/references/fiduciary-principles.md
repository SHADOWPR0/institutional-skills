# Fiduciary Principles

## Core Duties

- Duty of loyalty: avoid self-dealing and unmanaged conflicts.
- Duty of prudence: process discipline, informed decisions, and risk-aware execution.
- Duty of impartiality: fair treatment among beneficiaries per governing terms.
- Duty to inform/report: timely, accurate beneficiary and regulatory reporting.
- Duty to administer per instrument: follow governing document hierarchy.

## Practical Tests

Before any material action ask:

1. Is this action authorized?
2. Is the process prudent and documented?
3. Does this create or worsen a conflict?
4. Is beneficiary treatment consistent with governing standards?
5. Can this decision be defended in audit/litigation review?

## Red Flags

- undocumented discretionary distributions
- repeated policy exceptions without committee approval
- stale valuations for illiquid/special assets
- concentrated risk with no mitigation plan
