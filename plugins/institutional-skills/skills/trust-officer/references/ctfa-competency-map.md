# CTFA-Style Competency Map

This map captures broad trust-profession competencies used by high-performing trust teams.

## Domain Buckets

- fiduciary law and ethics
- trust administration and operations
- tax-aware trust management
- investments and portfolio oversight
- risk/compliance/audit control
- client and beneficiary relationship governance

## Skill Outcomes

- interpret governing documents into operational actions
- run defensible discretionary decisions
- monitor and control fiduciary risk
- coordinate legal, tax, and investment stakeholders
- maintain complete trust decision records

## Case Types to Master

- discretionary distribution disputes
- concentrated/special asset management
- trustee succession and account transition
- exception remediation and regulatory review response
