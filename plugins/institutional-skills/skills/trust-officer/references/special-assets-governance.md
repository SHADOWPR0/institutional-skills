# Special Assets Governance

## Asset Types

- closely held business interests
- real estate and operating properties
- mineral, royalty, IP, or litigation assets
- hard-to-value private investments

## Control Framework

- independent valuation cadence and methodology
- conflict-of-interest screening and approvals
- liquidity planning for taxes/distributions/expenses
- event-based review triggers (capital calls, covenant stress, legal actions)

## Risk Triggers

- stale valuation beyond policy window
- missing management information rights
- unresolved legal/title issues
- outsized portfolio concentration without mitigation
