# Trust Risk Controls

## Risk Domains

- fiduciary breach risk
- operational and settlement risk
- tax/compliance deadline risk
- valuation and model risk
- conduct and beneficiary dispute risk

## Control Stack

- maker-checker for material actions
- authority matrix with approval thresholds
- monthly exception review committee
- quarterly account-level risk scoring
- annual control self-assessment and remediation plan

## Key Risk Indicators

- unresolved policy exceptions > SLA
- late or corrected beneficiary reports
- missing valuation updates
- unapproved discretionary distributions
