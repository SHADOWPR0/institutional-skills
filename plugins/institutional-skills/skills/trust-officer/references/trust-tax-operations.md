# Trust Tax Operations

## Coverage

- annual filing workflow and calendar controls
- DNI-sensitive distribution coordination
- realized gain/loss and carryforward tracking
- jurisdiction and filing nexus monitoring

## Operational Controls

- tax data checklist with completeness status
- filing and extension deadlines with owner assignment
- variance review vs prior year and budget assumptions
- post-filing issue log and remediation tracking

## Metrics

- on-time filing rate
- unresolved tax exceptions
- realized-vs-projected taxable income variance
- distribution timing exceptions impacting tax outcome

## Escalation

Escalate to licensed tax professionals when legal interpretation, ambiguous treatment, or regulatory disputes appear.
