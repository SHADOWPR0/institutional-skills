# Commercial Contract Review Framework

## Core Clauses

- scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria
- fees, payment terms, and audit rights
- IP ownership and license boundaries
- confidentiality and data protection obligations
- limitation of liability and indemnities
- termination rights and transition obligations

## Risk Controls

- redline priority by business impact
- fallback clause positions (A/B/C)
- approval thresholds for non-standard terms

## Output

- contract risk summary
- must-fix / should-fix / accept-with-control list
- negotiation strategy and walk-away points
