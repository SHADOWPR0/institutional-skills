# Legal Intake and Risk Triage

## Intake Fields

- issue summary and business objective
- parties, jurisdictions, deadlines
- relevant documents and authority matrix
- prior related matters and open disputes

## Triage Matrix

- severity: low / moderate / high / critical
- likelihood: unlikely / possible / probable
- reversibility: reversible / difficult / irreversible
- urgency: normal / time-sensitive / emergency

## Immediate Escalation Triggers

- regulatory inquiry or enforcement action
- threatened litigation or formal demand
- unclear authority on high-value commitments
- privacy/security incidents with legal exposure
