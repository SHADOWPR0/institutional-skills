# Mortgage Playbook

Borrower-level and securitization-level mortgage underwriting.

## 1) Residential Borrower-Level Underwriting

### Core Dimensions
- income quality and stability
- debt-to-income and residual income
- credit behavior (not only score)
- LTV/CLTV, occupancy, and reserves
- property appraisal quality and marketability

### Product Contexts
- agency-conforming
- jumbo
- non-QM / DSCR investor loans
- portfolio and bridge products

### Failure Modes
- income misrepresentation
- appraisal inflation
- payment shock / rate reset risk
- weak servicing quality

## 2) Commercial Mortgage / CRE Borrower-Level Underwriting

- property NOI stability
- DSCR and debt yield
- sponsor net worth and liquidity
- lease rollover risk
- capex and tenant quality

## 3) MBS / RMBS / CMBS Structuring and Syndication

### Pool Analysis
- collateral stratification and concentration
- prepayment, delinquency, and default behavior
- loss severity and recovery lag assumptions

### Structure Design
- tranche subordination and credit enhancement
- trigger framework (OC/IC/events)
- reserve accounts and servicing advances
- reps/warrants and put-back exposure

### Distribution Focus
- investor class fit by tranche profile
- spread/rating calibration under stress
- liquidity and mark sensitivity

## 4) Mortgage Watchlist Triggers

- delinquency roll-rate acceleration
- vintage underperformance
- geographic or channel concentration stress
- servicer deterioration indicators
