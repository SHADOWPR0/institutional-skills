## Escalation Rules

- Add Legal/Regulatory specialist when cross-border, bankruptcy-remote, or sanctions-sensitive features appear.
- Add Liquidity specialist when maturity mismatch or funding fragility dominates.
- Add Workout/Restructuring specialist when downside case shows principal impairment risk.
- Auto-escalate to compliance when fees, collection mechanics, or rates may violate local law.
- Add Industry Vertical Specialist when sector dynamics materially drive valuation or default assumptions.
