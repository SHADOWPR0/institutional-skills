# Insurance and Risk Transfer Playbook

Framework for insurance program design, captive strategy, reinsurance, and risk transfer overlays.

## 1) Coverage

- corporate insurance program architecture
- captive design and retention strategy
- reinsurance structure and treaty optimization
- insurance-linked/risk-transfer interfaces for large exposures

## 2) Core Formulas

- **Loss Ratio** = Incurred Losses / Earned Premium
- **Expense Ratio** = Underwriting Expenses / Earned Premium
- **Combined Ratio** = Loss Ratio + Expense Ratio
- **Retention Ratio** = Net Retained Risk / Gross Risk Written
- **Reserve Adequacy Gap** = Required Reserves - Booked Reserves
- **Risk Transfer Efficiency** = (Volatility Reduction) / (Transfer Cost)

## 3) Underwriting/Decision Priorities

- tail-risk profile and correlation with core operations
- attachment/limit structure by peril and severity
- counterparty credit quality of carriers/reinsurers
- claims process quality and time-to-recovery
- legal/regulatory compliance of coverage language

## 4) Structural Controls

- clear coverage triggers and exclusions review
- concentration limits by carrier and peril
- collateralized reinsurance terms when needed
- regular reserve and catastrophe-model validation

## 5) Stress Testing

- severe event and aggregate loss scenarios
- counterparty impairment in stressed markets
- coverage dispute/timing lag scenario
- premium inflation and capacity withdrawal scenario
