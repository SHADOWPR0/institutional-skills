# Banking And Credit Research Lifecycle

## Boundary

This process keeps banking, credit, underwriting, lender, and deal-structure
guidance current. It may improve sources, checklists, scenarios, routing, and
failure detection. It may not silently change underwriting math, risk
definitions, legal conclusions, credit policy, or production behavior.

## Sources

Use existing local references first, then free primary sources: Federal Reserve,
OCC, FDIC, FFIEC, CFPB, SEC, SBA, IRS, applicable state regulators, statutes,
rules, official guidance, lender/program primary materials, rating-agency or
original institutional credit research, and rigorous original studies.

Every jurisdiction-sensitive item records jurisdiction, publication date,
effective date, as-of date, supersession status, and retrieval date. Secondary
commentary can nominate a source but cannot support a rule or decision by itself.

## Bounded Cycle

Per weekly cycle:

- nominate at most 20 items
- reject exact, semantic, and mechanism duplicates first
- deep-review at most 6
- promote at most 2 material updates
- require direct provenance, a falsifiable operational implication, and a mapped
  underwriting scenario
- reject generic commentary, recycled summaries, unavailable evidence, and
  changes that merely add words

Zero promotions is success when nothing material clears.

## Promotion

Auto-promotable after all tests pass: source/supersession repairs, clearer
decision guidance, regression fixtures, and safe routing/failure-detection
repairs. Stage changes to calculations, credit policy, legal conclusions,
capital/risk policy, production systems, or domain ownership for the principal.

Tax remains lightweight issue spotting. Route through `universal-banker`, use
`tax-strategy` as support, and identify when current professional tax or legal
review is required.

## Cadence And Scorecard

- Weekly: bounded scout and source integrity/freshness.
- Monthly: expiry/supersession and scenario regression.
- Quarterly: full benchmark synthesis and ownership audit.
- Event: material law, rule, program, methodology, or user-supplied source.

Track freshness, authoritative-source ratio, duplicate/rejection rate, scenario
pass rate, stale references removed, behavior improved, ownership conflicts,
token delta, and unresolved gaps.
