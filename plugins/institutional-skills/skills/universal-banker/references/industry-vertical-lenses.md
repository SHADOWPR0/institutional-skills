# Industry Vertical Lenses

Sector overlays to force realistic assumptions in underwriting, valuation, and structure design.

## How to Use

- Select one primary sector and one secondary exposure sector.
- Apply the sector risk drivers to cash-flow, collateral, and valuation assumptions.
- Add at least one sector-specific downside scenario to every recommendation.

## Vertical Playbook Matrix

## 1) Technology and Software
- key drivers: retention/churn, ARR quality, gross margin durability, platform risk
- pitfalls: growth-duration overvaluation, customer concentration, AI cost inflation

## 2) Healthcare and Life Sciences
- key drivers: reimbursement mix, regulatory path, payer concentration, clinical risk
- pitfalls: binary trial outcomes, coding/regulatory reset, litigation risk

## 3) Financials and Fintech
- key drivers: credit quality, funding stability, CAC/LTV, fraud losses
- pitfalls: regulatory intervention, run risk, underwriting model drift

## 4) Energy and Natural Resources
- key drivers: commodity curves, lifting costs, reserve quality, hedging policy
- pitfalls: basis blowouts, capex overruns, policy/regulatory shock

## 5) Industrials and Manufacturing
- key drivers: order backlog quality, utilization, input-cost pass-through, working capital
- pitfalls: cyclicality underestimation, supplier fragility, execution bottlenecks

## 6) Transportation and Logistics
- key drivers: load factors, route economics, fuel and maintenance cost sensitivity
- pitfalls: utilization collapse, asset residual markdown, labor disruption

## 7) Consumer and Retail
- key drivers: same-store trends, margin mix, inventory turns, brand strength
- pitfalls: discounting spiral, channel conflict, inventory obsolescence

## 8) Real Estate and Infrastructure
- key drivers: occupancy, lease rollover, capex burden, cost-of-capital
- pitfalls: refinancing cliff, valuation lag, climate/physical risk

## 9) Telecom and Media
- key drivers: churn, ARPU, capex intensity, spectrum/licensing constraints
- pitfalls: technology transition cost, regulatory pricing pressure

## 10) Public Sector and Municipal
- key drivers: tax/revenue elasticity, legal pledge security, reserve quality
- pitfalls: political constraints, delayed corrective action, pension overhang

## 11) Agriculture and Food Systems
- key drivers: yield volatility, weather risk, input costs, commodity pricing
- pitfalls: climate shock, concentration in buyers/offtakers, biosecurity events

## 12) Frontier and Emerging-Market Contexts
- key drivers: FX stability, legal enforceability, sovereign transfer risk
- pitfalls: convertibility restrictions, governance gaps, sudden policy shifts
