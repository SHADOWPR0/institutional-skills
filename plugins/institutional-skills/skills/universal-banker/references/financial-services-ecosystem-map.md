# Financial Services Ecosystem Map

Master map for spawning specialist agents across the entire finance stack.

## 1) Banking and Credit Stack

- Corporate/Commercial Lending
- CRE and Mortgage
- Equipment and Leasing
- Private Banking and Specialty Lending
- Structured Credit and Securitization
- Public Finance and Municipal
- Microfinance and Inclusive Lending

## 2) Markets and Advisory Stack

- Capital Markets Underwriting (ECM/DCM/LF)
- Syndication and Distribution
- M&A and Corporate Advisory
- Market Making and Liquidity
- Structured Products Engineering

## 3) Private Capital Stack

- Private Credit
- Private Equity / Buyout Underwriting
- NAV/Subscription Facilities
- Special Situations / Distressed

## 4) Asset Management Stack

- Multi-asset allocation and portfolio construction
- Risk budgeting and policy compliance
- Manager/factor oversight and attribution
- Liquidity sleeve governance

## 5) Risk, Compliance, and Control Stack

- Enterprise Risk Governance
- Legal/Regulatory/Compliance
- Model Risk and Validation
- Portfolio and Concentration Governance
- Workout and Restructuring

## 6) Treasury, Payments, and Balance-Sheet Stack

- ALM / Liquidity and Funding
- FX and Rate Risk Management
- Payments and Working-Capital rails
- Cash concentration and treasury operations

## 7) Insurance and Risk Transfer Stack

- Insurance program design
- Captive structures
- Reinsurance strategy
- ILS/cat risk transfer interfaces

## 8) Agent-Spawning Template

For each specialist agent, define:

- mandate and objective
- explicit scope boundaries
- required formulas/metrics
- data requirements and quality gates
- output format and deadline
- escalation triggers and decline criteria

## 9) Orchestration Rules

- always include Enterprise Risk Governor for material decisions
- add Industry Vertical Specialist when sector dynamics drive assumptions
- use parallel specialist runs, then reconcile assumptions in one IC memo
- log assumption conflicts and assign owner + resolution deadline
