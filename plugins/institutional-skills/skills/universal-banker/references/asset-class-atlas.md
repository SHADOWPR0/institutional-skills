# Asset-Class Atlas (Core + Fringe)

Use this atlas to map unusual opportunities into underwriting lanes with the right control stack.
For full-stack role coverage beyond underwriting (advisory, treasury, risk transfer, controls), see `references/financial-services-ecosystem-map.md`.

## 1) Corporate and Bank Credit

### Coverage
- C&I revolvers/term loans
- ABL (inventory/receivables/equipment)
- bridge and acquisition facilities
- sponsor-backed leveraged credit

### Dominant Risks
- cash-flow compression
- refinancing wall
- collateral leakage

### Must-Have Controls
- maintenance covenants or tight incurrence package
- borrowing-base disciplines (for ABL)
- restricted payments and debt baskets with hard tests

## 2) Real Estate and Mortgage

### Coverage
- residential first liens, non-QM/DSCR, portfolio products
- CRE bridge, construction, stabilized term loans
- SASB and conduit execution

### Dominant Risks
- valuation error
- lease-roll/refi risk
- borrower payment shock / servicer weakness

### Must-Have Controls
- DSCR/debt-yield minimums
- reserves (TI/LC/capex/taxes/insurance)
- trigger-driven cash traps and collateral performance tests

## 3) Securitization and Structured Credit

### Coverage
- RMBS, CMBS, ABS, CLO
- whole loan sales and forward-flow channels
- warehouse-to-term takeout structures

### Dominant Risks
- collateral drift before close
- trigger fragility
- warehouse rollover/liquidity gaps

### Must-Have Controls
- robust OC/IC and reserve design
- eligibility criteria and substitution limits
- legal isolation and servicing replacement mechanics

## 4) Capital Markets Underwriting

### Coverage
- IPO/follow-on/convertible
- IG/HY bonds and leveraged loans
- preferreds, hybrids, structured notes

### Dominant Risks
- demand quality overestimation
- spread/price gap risk during launch
- underwriting hold accumulation

### Must-Have Controls
- pre-defined flex windows
- hard hold limits and de-risk hedging plan
- investor segmentation and fallback placement map

## 5) Private Capital

### Coverage
- PE/LBO financing
- private credit (senior, unitranche, mezz, specialty)
- NAV/subscription lines

### Dominant Risks
- pro forma optimism
- weak documentation in competitive process
- sponsor misalignment under stress

### Must-Have Controls
- downside covenant architecture
- lender control rights and amendment discipline
- concentration and vintage caps at portfolio level

## 6) Private Banking and Specialty Lending

### Coverage
- securities-backed lending / Lombard lines
- crypto-backed lending and margin facilities
- hard money real-estate bridge lending
- merchant cash advance and revenue-based advances
- bespoke UHNW bridge and collateralized private lines

### Dominant Risks
- collateral gap risk and liquidation lag
- legal/regulatory usury and disclosure breaches
- borrower dependency on refinancing or asset inflation

### Must-Have Controls
- dynamic haircut ladders and intraday margin-call protocols
- hard legal-compliance checks by jurisdiction
- enforceable collateral and collection playbooks with audit trails

## 7) Asset and Equipment Finance / Leasing

### Coverage
- equipment loans and master lease programs
- aircraft finance and operating/finance leasing
- truck and fleet finance
- marine and rail assets
- private yacht/jet secured facilities

### Dominant Risks
- residual value markdowns and obsolescence
- utilization collapse and maintenance deferral
- repossession and remarketing friction

### Must-Have Controls
- conservative residual assumptions with stress haircuts
- maintenance reserve and inspection regimes
- legal perfection and swift enforcement rights

## 8) Market Making and Liquidity Provision

### Coverage
- equities/futures/options market making
- ETF/AP and basis/arbitrage support
- crypto centralized/decentralized venue quoting

### Dominant Risks
- adverse selection/toxicity
- latency and stale-quote loss
- venue/outage/settlement disruption

### Must-Have Controls
- inventory and loss limits by regime
- toxicity and quote-pull thresholds
- failover venues and hedge-continuity playbook

## 9) Project, Infrastructure, and Real Assets

### Coverage
- power/utilities, transport, digital infra
- aviation/shipping finance
- commodity trade and inventory finance

### Dominant Risks
- construction/commissioning slippage
- offtake/counterparty concentration
- residual value mismatch

### Must-Have Controls
- completion guarantees and contingency reserves
- DSRA/LLCR/PLCR-style structural tests
- covenant packages tied to utilization/cash sweep triggers

## 10) Public and Municipal Finance

### Coverage
- general obligation and revenue-backed debt
- project-backed municipal structures
- agency and authority funding programs

### Dominant Risks
- tax-base deterioration
- political/regulatory reset
- legal priority ambiguities

### Must-Have Controls
- conservative revenue stress tests
- reserve/intercept mechanics
- legal priority verification and covenant clarity

## 11) M&A and Corporate Advisory

### Coverage
- buy-side and sell-side M&A
- carve-outs, spin-offs, and strategic alternatives
- fairness/opinion support and transaction structuring
- integration value-capture planning

### Dominant Risks
- synergy overstatement
- weak integration execution
- financing certainty and closing-risk gaps

### Must-Have Controls
- conservative downside valuation range
- antitrust/regulatory gating with timeline stress
- explicit walk-away and reprice conditions

## 12) Microfinance and Inclusive Finance

### Coverage
- individual and group microloans
- MFI warehouse and term facilities
- SME transition lending from micro-to-formal banking
- social enterprise and blended-finance structures

### Dominant Risks
- borrower income volatility and climate shocks
- group contagion/default clustering
- operational fraud and weak servicing capacity

### Must-Have Controls
- cohort-level vintage analysis and PAR monitoring
- portfolio caps by region, crop, and borrower type
- transparent pricing and consumer-protection compliance

## 13) Treasury and Payments

### Coverage
- ALM and liquidity management programs
- payment-rail strategy and cash concentration design
- FX and rates hedging overlays

### Dominant Risks
- funding concentration and rollover fragility
- settlement outages and operational losses
- hedge ineffectiveness and basis shocks

### Must-Have Controls
- stress liquidity buffers and contingency funding plans
- payment exception governance and fraud controls
- hedge-policy limits and counterparty concentration controls

## 14) Insurance and Risk Transfer

### Coverage
- insurance program architecture
- captive and reinsurance design
- catastrophe and large-loss transfer overlays

### Dominant Risks
- reserve inadequacy and coverage gap risk
- carrier/reinsurer counterparty risk
- pricing/capacity shocks after large events

### Must-Have Controls
- periodic reserve and model validation
- diversified risk-transfer panel
- clear trigger/exclusion governance

## 15) Esoteric and Fringe Collateral

### Coverage
- music/media royalty streams
- litigation finance portfolios
- franchise/merchant cash-flow advances
- carbon/environmental credit inventories
- IP/data-contract receivables
- catastrophe/insurance-linked structures

### Dominant Risks
- model instability and data scarcity
- legal enforceability uncertainty
- correlation spikes hidden by short history

### Must-Have Controls
- conservative advance rates/haircuts
- granular concentration limits
- independent data validation and hard stop-loss governance

## 16) Asset Management and Portfolio Governance

### Coverage
- strategic/tactical asset allocation
- portfolio risk budgeting and drawdown governance
- manager and factor oversight

### Dominant Risks
- hidden concentration and crowding
- regime shift invalidating historical assumptions
- liquidity mismatch in stressed exits

### Must-Have Controls
- explicit IPS constraints and hard risk limits
- attribution-driven review cadence
- liquidity sleeve and stress-based rebalance rules

## Lane Selection Quick Rules

- **Cash-flow lender risk:** C&I / CRE / Private Credit lead.
- **Specialty collateralized lending (SBL/crypto/hard money/MCA):** Private Banking & Specialty Lending lead.
- **Residual-value and lease economics risk:** Asset & Equipment Finance lead.
- **Collateral + waterfall risk:** Securitization Engineer lead.
- **Control transaction or strategic process:** M&A and Corporate Advisory lead.
- **Placement/tape risk:** Capital Markets + Syndication co-lead.
- **Two-sided quoting risk:** Market Making lead.
- **Balance-sheet liquidity or payment rail redesign:** Treasury & Payments Strategist lead.
- **Risk transfer/captive/reinsurance design:** Insurance & Risk Transfer Specialist lead.
- **Portfolio-level allocation and risk-budget work:** Asset Management and Portfolio Strategy lead.
- **Public-sector repayment source:** Public Finance Specialist lead.
- **Micro-borrower or MFI portfolio exposure:** Microfinance Specialist lead.
- **Novel/esoteric assets:** Esoteric Specialist + Enterprise Risk mandatory.
