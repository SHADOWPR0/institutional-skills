# Asset Management and Portfolio Strategy Playbook

Governance framework for portfolio construction, risk budgeting, and manager/factor oversight.

## 1) Coverage

- strategic and tactical asset allocation
- portfolio risk-budget and drawdown controls
- manager selection and mandate monitoring
- factor overlays and benchmark-relative governance

## 2) Core Formulas

- **Sharpe Ratio** = (Portfolio Return - Risk-Free Rate) / Volatility
- **Information Ratio** = Active Return / Tracking Error
- **Max Drawdown** = Max(Peak-to-Trough Decline)
- **Tracking Error** = StdDev(Portfolio Return - Benchmark Return)
- **Risk Contribution_i** = Weight_i * Marginal Contribution to Portfolio Risk
- **Hit Ratio** = Winning Periods / Total Periods

## 3) Decision Priorities

- explicit objective hierarchy (return, drawdown, liquidity, liability match)
- regime-aware allocation assumptions
- concentration and correlation management
- rebalance discipline and transaction-cost awareness

## 4) Governance Controls

- investment policy statement (IPS) constraints encoded in decisions
- hard risk limits (drawdown, TE, liquidity sleeves)
- manager watchlist triggers and replacement protocol
- post-trade attribution and assumption audit

## 5) Stress and Monitoring

- macro regime stress (rate, inflation, growth shocks)
- liquidity-gap stress for private/illiquid sleeves
- factor crowding and correlation-break stress
- monthly attribution and quarterly policy compliance review
