# Municipal Finance Playbook

Underwriting framework for municipal and public-sector credit.

## 1) Deal Types

- general obligation (GO) bonds
- essential-service and utility revenue bonds
- transportation/toll and airport/port finance
- healthcare/higher-ed authority debt
- special tax, assessment, and public authority structures

## 2) Core Formulas

- **DSCR** = Net Revenue Available for Debt Service / Annual Debt Service
- **Debt Burden** = Net Direct Debt / Taxable Assessed Value (or population proxy)
- **Pension Burden** = Unfunded Pension Liability / Revenue
- **Liquidity Days** = Unrestricted Cash / (Operating Expense / 365)
- **Coverage Cushion** = (Base DSCR - Minimum Covenant DSCR)
- **Refinancing Exposure** = Debt Maturing in Next N Years / Total Debt

## 3) Base Metric Ranges (Context-Dependent)

- utility/essential-service revenue DSCR often targeted >= 1.25x to 1.50x
- higher-volatility/project-style revenue often needs higher coverage cushions
- liquidity targets vary by issuer volatility and legal flexibility
- concentration limits required for narrow tax or payer bases

## 4) Underwriting Focus

- legal security and priority of pledge
- revenue durability under recession and policy shifts
- demographic and tax-base trajectory
- fixed-cost rigidity and political willingness to adjust rates/taxes
- capex backlog and deferred maintenance burden

## 5) Structure and Covenant Controls

- additional bonds test (ABT) calibrated to stressed coverage
- reserve requirements and intercept/trustee mechanics
- rate covenant and periodic reset provisions (where applicable)
- restricted transfers and debt-incurrence guardrails
- event-of-default and remedies clarity

## 6) Stress Framework

- revenue shock (volume + rate + collection compression)
- delayed rate-case/political intervention scenario
- inflation stress on O&M and capex
- refinancing window closure and higher-rate reset
- climate/physical risk for exposed geographies and assets

## 7) Monitoring

- monthly/quarterly collection and coverage tracking
- budget variance and reserve draw trends
- capital plan execution and project-delay flags
- covenant compliance and legal/tax-policy developments
