# Sub-Agent Orchestration

Use this framework to split complex deals into specialist lanes.

## 1) When to Spawn Specialists

Spawn specialists when any are true:

- multi-asset or multi-jurisdiction structure
- securitization + balance-sheet + distribution dependencies
- thin covenant protections with high leverage
- execution timing risk dominates underwriting quality
- novel or data-sparse collateral model drives economics
- specialty-lending terms may trigger legal/compliance constraints

## 3) Per-Specialist Brief Template

- mandate and objective
- scope boundaries
- assumptions
- required output
- hard constraints
- top red flags
- escalation triggers

## 4) Reconciliation Protocol

After specialist outputs:

1. normalize assumptions across lanes
2. resolve conflicts (especially covenant, collateral, and distribution assumptions)
3. update downside case to reflect weakest-link risk
4. produce final IC memo and decision
5. tag unresolved assumptions and assign follow-up owners with deadlines

## 5) Quality Gate

No final recommendation unless:

- every mandatory lane submitted output
- critical assumptions are aligned
- downside and liquidity risks are explicitly priced/mitigated
- legal and operational execution paths are feasible on required timeline
