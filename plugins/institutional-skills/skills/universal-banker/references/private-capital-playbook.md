# Private Capital Playbook (PE + Private Credit + VC Handoffs)

Underwriting framework for sponsor-backed and private-market structures.

For transaction-process and valuation execution, pair with `references/ma-advisory-playbook.md`.
For venture investing, growth equity, founder/platform support, reserves, and
portfolio-fit judgment, route first to
`{SKILLS_ROOT}/investment-management` and its
`references/venture-capital-operating-desk.md` (not bundled; recipient resource required). This banker playbook owns the
credit, M&A, financing, covenants, lender, and deal-structure parts.

## Private Equity Deal Underwriting

### Core Questions
- Is value creation operational or multiple expansion fiction?
- Is leverage a tool or a crutch?
- Is downside survivable without perfect execution?

### Diligence Focus
- market structure and competitive moat
- management capability and incentive alignment
- realistic base case vs sponsor case
- exit optionality under conservative assumptions

## Private Credit Underwriting

### Product Coverage
- senior secured / unitranche
- mezzanine
- asset-based and specialty finance
- NAV and subscription facilities

### Key Underwriting Pillars
- downside protection and collateral enforceability
- covenant architecture and lender control rights
- sponsor support probability
- refinancing and maturity risk

## Portfolio Construction

- vintage and sector diversification
- sponsor concentration limits
- hold size and reserve policy
- liquidity planning and denominator risk controls

## Venture Capital / Growth Equity Handoff

Use this lane when a venture or growth company needs banking judgment:

- venture debt, revenue-based finance, asset-backed lending, working-capital
  lines, warehouse facilities, subscription/NAV facilities, or bridge loans
- M&A, strategic alternatives, recapitalization, secondary sale, or exit process
- term-sheet, liquidation preference, protective provisions, warrants, covenants,
  collateral, intercreditor, or closing-condition review
- capital-markets readiness, crossover financing, private placements,
  syndication, or lender/investor routing

Return a banker packet with structure, pricing, covenants, collateral, closing
conditions, downside controls, and monitoring. `investment-management` then
decides whether the opportunity belongs in the portfolio and how much capital or
reserve budget it deserves.

## Monitoring and Workout

- monthly covenant and KPI monitoring
- trigger-based intervention framework
- amendment discipline
- recovery-maximizing workout process
