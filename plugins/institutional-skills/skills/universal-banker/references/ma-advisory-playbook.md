# M&A and Corporate Advisory Playbook

Execution and risk framework for buy-side, sell-side, carve-out, and strategic alternatives.

## 1) Coverage

- buy-side M&A (strategic and sponsor)
- sell-side process management
- carve-outs and spin-offs
- merger of equals and transformative combinations
- fairness/opinion support and transaction certainty analysis

## 2) Core Formulas

- **Enterprise Value (EV)** = Equity Value + Net Debt + Preferred + Minority Interest
- **Equity Value** = EV - Net Debt - Other Senior Claims
- **Synergy NPV** = PV(Post-Tax Synergies) - One-Time Integration Costs
- **Accretion/(Dilution)** = (Pro Forma EPS - Standalone EPS) / Standalone EPS
- **Implied Acquisition Multiple** = Purchase Consideration / Target EBITDA (or EBIT)
- **Deleveraging Path** = (Net Debt_t / EBITDA_t) over forecast years
- **Break Fee Burden** = Break Fee / Transaction Equity Value

## 3) Process Stages

1. thesis and target/buyer universe framing
2. valuation range and downside triangulation
3. diligence and red-flag surfacing
4. structure and financing certainty design
5. negotiation, signing, and closing-risk governance
6. post-close integration tracking

## 4) Underwriting Priorities

- standalone quality of earnings and cash conversion
- credible synergy sources and timing
- integration complexity and management bandwidth
- antitrust/regulatory/foreign-investment constraints
- financing certainty (debt/ECM/DCM market windows and flex risk)

## 5) Industry-Adjusted Diligence

- apply `references/industry-vertical-lenses.md` to normalize assumptions
- require sector-specific downside cases, not generic blended scenarios
- build vertical-specific KPI bridge from diligence to valuation

## 6) Common Failure Modes

- paying for perfect execution
- overestimating cost synergies and ignoring revenue dis-synergies
- weak carve-out TSA assumptions and stranded cost leakage
- underestimating regulatory timeline and remedy risk

## 7) Outputs Required

- valuation bridge (base, downside, severe downside)
- financing and covenant capacity memo
- closing-risk map and contingency plan
- 100-day integration risk register with KPI ownership
