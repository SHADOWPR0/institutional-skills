# Underwriting Core

Universal framework used across all banking and credit verticals.

For reusable formulas and baseline metric bands, use `references/formulas-metrics-compendium.md`.

## 1) Deal Framing

- What is being financed?
- Who bears first-loss risk?
- What is the true source of repayment?
- What fails first in the downside case?

## 2) Borrower and Sponsor Assessment

- business durability and cyclicality
- governance and execution quality
- leverage tolerance and refinancing dependence
- sponsor behavior, support history, and alignment

## 3) Cash-Flow Underwriting

- base-case free cash-flow capacity
- debt service coverage under stress
- working-capital and seasonality risk
- capex and maintenance vs growth spending realism

## 4) Collateral and Security Package

- collateral quality, legal perfection, lien priority
- valuation methodology and haircut reasonableness
- liquidation friction, timeline, and recovery confidence

## 5) Structural Protection

- covenants (maintenance/incurrence, springing triggers)
- cash sweeps, reserves, borrowing base controls
- restricted payments and debt incurrence limits
- change-of-control and default frameworks

## 6) Pricing and Return

- expected loss vs spread/fee compensation
- capital usage and risk-adjusted return
- carry, mark-to-market, and liquidity premiums

## 7) Downside and Stress Testing

- revenue shock + margin compression + higher rates
- refinancing shut window
- collateral markdown + slower liquidation
- correlated tail event and counterparty stress

## 8) Decision Protocol

- Approve only if:
  - downside can be survived with acceptable loss
  - legal/covenant package can enforce lender control
  - risk-adjusted return compensates uncertainty

- Otherwise:
  - reprice
  - restructure
  - reduce size
  - decline

## 9) Monitoring Blueprint

- monthly borrower health metrics
- covenant and trigger dashboard
- collateral and market liquidity checks
- watchlist escalation and action playbook

## 10) IC Memo Template

- transaction summary
- edge and risks
- structure and pricing terms
- stress case outcomes
- conditions precedent
- post-close monitoring plan
