# Securitization and Syndication Playbook

Framework for designing and distributing structured credit and syndicated transactions.

## Securitization Coverage

- RMBS
- CMBS
- ABS (consumer, auto, card, esoteric)
- CLO (broadly syndicated and middle market)

## Structuring Principles

- isolate asset risk from originator risk where possible
- size enhancement to survive stressed collateral outcomes
- align triggers to leading indicators, not lagging defaults
- avoid complexity that cannot be monitored in real time

## Waterfall Design Checklist

- priority of payments
- OC/IC tests and cure mechanics
- reserve accounts and release conditions
- servicer replacement triggers
- clean-up calls and optional redemption terms

## Syndication Strategy

- pre-sounding and anchor demand mapping
- tranche sizing by investor base
- spread/rating trade-off calibration
- flex protocol and fallback execution plan

## Risk Controls

- warehouse line rollover risk
- collateral quality drift between ramp and closing
- basis and hedge slippage
- legal and operational transfer risk
