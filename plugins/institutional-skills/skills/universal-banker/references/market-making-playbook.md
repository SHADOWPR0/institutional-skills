# Market-Making Playbook

Coverage for traditional and digital market making in an underwriting-aware risk framework.

## 1) Traditional Market Making

- quote both sides with inventory-aware skew
- manage adverse selection, latency, and queue-position risk
- hedge inventory promptly with correlated instruments
- size participation to liquidity regime

### Metrics
- realized spread vs quoted spread
- adverse selection loss
- inventory VaR and turnover
- hedge slippage

## 2) Crypto and Digital Market Making

- account for venue fragility and API instability
- include basis/funding interaction in inventory economics
- enforce per-venue and cross-venue limits
- throttle quotes during exchange stress events

## 3) Governance

- hard stop-loss and max inventory
- quote pull rules for toxicity spikes
- playbook for exchange outages and failed hedges
