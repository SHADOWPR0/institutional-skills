# Microfinance Playbook

Underwriting and portfolio governance for micro-borrowers and MFI-level facilities.

## 1) Product and Counterparty Coverage

- individual microloans
- group lending / village banking
- SME graduation loans
- MFI warehouse and term facilities
- blended-finance and guarantee-supported micro portfolios

## 2) Core Formulas

- **PAR30** = Outstanding Principal >30 days past due / Gross Loan Portfolio
- **PAR90** = Outstanding Principal >90 days past due / Gross Loan Portfolio
- **Write-Off Ratio** = Loans Written Off / Average Gross Loan Portfolio
- **Collection Efficiency** = Amount Collected / Amount Due
- **Yield on Portfolio** = Interest and Fee Income / Average Gross Loan Portfolio
- **OER (Operating Expense Ratio)** = Operating Expenses / Average Gross Loan Portfolio
- **FSS (Financial Self-Sufficiency)** = Adjusted Operating Revenue / (Financial Expense + Loan Loss Provision + Operating Expense)
- **Client Retention** = Repeat Borrowers / Eligible Matured Borrowers

## 3) Metric Bands (Portfolio-Dependent)

- PAR30 and PAR90 must be trend-monitored by cohort, geography, and channel
- write-off policy should be explicit and consistently applied
- OER and FSS thresholds set by model (urban/rural, ticket size, servicing model)
- concentration limits by region, sector, and borrower segment

## 4) Underwriting Focus

- cash-flow reality of borrower households and micro-businesses
- climate/seasonality sensitivity
- repayment culture and group-dynamics quality
- field-operations quality and fraud controls
- pricing fairness and customer protection compliance

## 5) Structure and Controls

- simple, transparent pricing and repayment terms
- borrower affordability checks and avoidance of debt cycling
- early-arrears intervention protocols
- robust agent/field audit trail and dual-control disbursement
- grievance/redress channels and conduct-risk governance

## 6) Stress Tests

- crop/weather shock and local demand collapse
- inflation shock on household staples and business inputs
- political intervention on collections or pricing
- fraud event scenario (branch/agent-level)

## 7) Monitoring and Escalation

- weekly arrears dashboard by branch/cohort
- restructuring vs write-off discipline
- borrower over-indebtedness indicators
- social-performance metrics alongside financial KPIs

## 8) Legal and Conduct Boundaries

- no coercive collection tactics
- no hidden-fee structures
- comply with local consumer-protection and microfinance laws
