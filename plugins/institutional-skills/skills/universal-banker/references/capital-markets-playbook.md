# Capital Markets Playbook

Coverage for underwriting and distribution across equity and debt capital markets.

## Equity Capital Markets (ECM)

### Use Cases
- IPO
- follow-on
- block trade
- convertibles-linked equity transactions

### Underwriting Focus
- issuer quality and earnings credibility
- valuation range and relative positioning
- investor demand quality (not just quantity)
- lock-up, overhang, and secondary flow risk

### Failure Modes
- weak book quality
- valuation overreach into risk-off tape
- forced stabilization risk

## Debt Capital Markets (DCM)

### Use Cases
- IG corporate debt
- HY debt
- convertibles
- preferreds and hybrids

### Underwriting Focus
- leverage and fixed-charge coverage durability
- maturity wall and refinancing risk
- covenant rigor and documentation quality
- use of proceeds and deleveraging realism

## Leveraged Finance / Syndicated Loans

### Use Cases
- TLB / institutional term loans
- revolver + term packages
- acquisition and recap financings

### Underwriting Focus
- first-loss position and covenant package
- flex risk and syndication certainty
- secondary market liquidity and break price risk

## Distribution and Syndication

- build investor segmentation map
- scenario test book-building outcomes
- set pricing/flex triggers before launch
- design fallback plans for weak tape

## Underwriter Risk Controls

- hard hold limits by name/sector/rating bucket
- stop-loss and de-risk protocols
- pre-committed flex and hedging framework
- balance-sheet capital and liquidity guardrails
