# Commercial Credit Playbook (CRE + C&I)

Focus on underwriting used by regional/community banks and larger balance-sheet lenders.

For specialty private-banking products (crypto-backed, hard money, MCA), see `references/private-banking-specialty-lending.md`.
For deeper equipment/lease underwriting, see `references/asset-equipment-finance-leasing.md`.

## CRE Underwriting

### Property and Cash-Flow Core
- stabilized NOI, lease rollover profile, and tenant concentration
- DSCR, debt yield, breakeven occupancy, and cap rate sensitivity
- sponsor support capacity and track record
- market vacancy and absorption dynamics

### Structure and Documentation
- recourse vs non-recourse terms
- amortization and extension tests
- cash management and lockbox controls
- reserve requirements (TI/LC/capex/taxes/insurance)

## C&I Underwriting

### Business Credit Core
- EBITDA quality and conversion to cash
- leverage path and fixed-charge coverage
- customer concentration and cyclicality
- inventory/receivables quality (for ABL)

### Structure and Controls
- collateral package and lien priority
- borrowing base mechanics and reporting cadence
- maintenance covenants and cure rights
- guarantor support and cross-default mapping

## Securities-Based Lending (SBL) and Margin Lending

- collateral eligibility and concentration limits
- haircut grid by asset type/liquidity
- margin call and liquidation protocol
- wrong-way risk controls

## Asset and Equipment Finance / Leasing

### Coverage
- equipment term loans and revolving equipment lines
- finance leases and operating leases
- sale-leaseback structures
- aircraft finance and leasing
- truck/fleet and trailer finance
- marine assets and private yacht/jet collateralized facilities

### Underwriting Core
- asset age, condition, maintenance, and obsolescence curve
- residual value realism and liquidation channel depth
- utilization profile and operator/lessee quality
- repossession/enforcement timeline by jurisdiction

### Core Metrics
- lease rate factor (periodic rent / equipment cost)
- residual value ratio (projected residual / original asset cost)
- loan-to-value and loan-to-orderly-liquidation-value
- debt service and fixed charge coverage of obligor
- remarketing loss assumptions in downside liquidation

### Structural Controls
- maintenance reserve framework and inspection rights
- assignment/consent controls on sublease transfers
- insurance naming lender/lessor as loss payee
- covenants for utilization, leverage, and minimum liquidity

## Portfolio Governance

- sector and obligor concentration limits
- risk-rating migration and reserve policy
- early-warning system and downgrade triggers
- workout and restructuring escalation
