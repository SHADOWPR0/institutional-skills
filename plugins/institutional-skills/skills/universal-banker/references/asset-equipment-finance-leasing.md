# Asset and Equipment Finance / Leasing Playbook

Underwriting guide for finance and operating leases, equipment loans, and asset-backed mobility finance.

## 1) Coverage

- equipment loans (industrial, medical, manufacturing, IT)
- finance leases and operating leases
- aircraft finance and leasing
- truck/fleet and trailer finance
- rail, marine, and specialty equipment
- private yacht and private jet secured facilities

## 2) Core Formulas

- **LTV** = Loan Balance / Current Asset Value
- **LTOLV** = Loan Balance / Orderly Liquidation Value
- **LTC** = Financing Amount / Total Acquisition Cost
- **Lease Rate Factor** = Periodic Lease Payment / Asset Cost
- **Residual Value Ratio** = Expected End-of-Term Value / Original Cost
- **Net Investment in Lease (simplified)** = PV(Lease Payments + Residual) - Initial Outlay
- **Break-Even Utilization** = (Fixed Costs + Debt/Lease Obligation) / Contribution Margin per Utilization Unit
- **Repossession Coverage** = Net Recovery After Costs / Outstanding Exposure

## 3) Default Policy Metric Bands (Internal Starting Points)

- heavy equipment base LTV often targeted <= 65-80% (asset/liquidity dependent)
- truck/fleet base LTV often targeted <= 60-75%
- aircraft base LTV often targeted <= 50-70% depending on type, age, and operator quality
- older/highly specialized assets require tighter LTV and shorter tenor
- residual value stress haircut should increase with asset age and market cyclicality

## 4) Underwriting Priorities

- asset quality, age, maintenance history, and technical condition
- secondary-market depth and dealer/broker liquidity
- operator/lessee cash-flow strength and utilization profile
- legal perfection, title, registration, and repossession enforceability
- insurance adequacy and loss-payee controls

## 5) Structural Controls

- maintenance reserves and inspection rights
- geographic/asset-type concentration caps
- covenant package: leverage, liquidity, utilization, and reporting
- accelerated amortization / cash sweep triggers when collateral quality deteriorates
- remarketing and residual-loss reserve policy

## 6) Stress Testing

- utilization drop and revenue compression
- residual value markdown (single-step and multi-step)
- maintenance cost inflation and downtime shock
- delayed repossession/remarketing scenario

## 7) Monitoring

- utilization and downtime dashboard by asset cohort
- maintenance and compliance covenant performance
- mark-to-market collateral quality trend
- recovery variance: modeled vs realized liquidation outcomes
