# Growth Skill Consolidation Report

## Consolidation Thesis

The library contains many useful growth-related skills, but they should not all be front doors. Most are connector, channel, or artifact skills. The canonical front door should be `growth-operating-system`.

## Canonical Roles

- `growth-operating-system`: strategy, architecture, measurement, prioritization.
- `ethical-supersuader`: high-integrity prose, investor/deal/founder writing, persuasion without manipulation.
- Connector skills: execution after strategy is defined.
- SEO/content skills: support modules for search/content work.
- CRM/sales automation skills: support modules for data access and workflow execution.

## Major Overlap Clusters

### CRM And Pipeline

Examples: HubSpot, Attio, Capsule, Pipedrive, Close, Zoho CRM, Salesmate, noCRM, CentralStationCRM, Dynamics.

Consolidation: keep as connectors; route CRM strategy through `growth-operating-system`.

### Email And Outreach

Examples: Instantly, Lemlist, ActiveCampaign, SendGrid, Postmark, SMTP2GO, MailerSend, BigMailer, EmailOctopus, Benchmark Email, Omnisend, Customer.io.

Consolidation: keep as channel execution tools; centralize segmentation, offer, compliance, copy, and metrics here.

### Lead Research And Enrichment

Examples: Apollo, Hunter, PhantomBuster, Aeroleads, Leadoku, lead-research-assistant.

Consolidation: centralize ICP and qualification rules here; tools fetch/enrich only.

### SEO, Content, And Entity

Examples: Ahrefs, SEMrush, on-page SEO, technical SEO, content quality, SEO writer, entity optimizer, internal linking, domain authority.

Consolidation: centralize growth strategy and measurement here; SEO skills perform channel-specific audits.

### Copy, Prose, Brand, Social

Examples: ethical-supersuader, beautiful_prose, content creator, brand analyzer, social media generator, Twitter optimizer.

Consolidation: use `ethical-supersuader` for quality prose and this skill for conversion/offer/funnel context.

## Deprecated As Front Doors

Do not delete these. Treat them as support modules:

- single-vendor CRM automation skills
- single-vendor email/SMS tools
- narrow SEO audit tools
- narrow brand/social/content generators
- duplicate lead research skills

## Upgrade Rule

Any growth-related task should first answer:

1. What bottleneck are we solving?
2. What customer segment?
3. What offer?
4. What channel?
5. What metric moves?
6. What is the kill switch?

Only then call tool-specific skills.
