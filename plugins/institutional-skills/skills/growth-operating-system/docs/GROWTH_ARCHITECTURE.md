# Growth Architecture

## North Star

Create durable, compounding growth by improving the full equation:

```text
Qualified Demand x Trust x Conversion x Retention x Referral Rate x Expansion
```

Reject vanity metrics unless they predict revenue, qualified pipeline, retained usage, or referrals.

## Layers

### Layer 1: Market Intelligence

Owns ICP, segmentation, market mapping, competitor analysis, offer gaps, pain extraction, objection mining, review mining, and demand analysis.

Outputs:

- ICP profiles
- customer archetypes
- pain libraries
- objection libraries
- opportunity maps
- qualification criteria

### Layer 2: Offer Engineering

Owns offer creation, offer stacking, pricing psychology, guarantee structures, value communication, risk reversal, and positioning.

Outputs:

- offer ladder
- offer stack
- proof map
- risk-reversal design
- pricing tests
- differentiation map

### Layer 3: Persuasion And Copy

Owns direct response, sales copy, landing pages, ads, email, SMS, scripts, and objection handling.

Use behavioral economics, Cialdini, Ogilvy, Hopkins, Schwartz, Hormozi, and modern performance marketing only when they increase clarity, relevance, proof, or decision confidence. Do not use them for manipulation.

Outputs:

- landing pages
- ad concepts
- email/SMS campaigns
- sales scripts
- objection replies
- campaign briefs

### Layer 4: Funnel Engineering

Owns lead capture, qualification, nurturing, appointment setting, routing, conversion paths, and CRM handoffs.

Outputs:

- funnel maps
- customer journeys
- nurture sequences
- lead scoring
- routing rules
- sales SLAs

### Layer 5: Growth Analytics

Owns CAC, LTV, payback, conversion rates, attribution, cohorts, channel performance, experimentation, and causal reasoning.

Outputs:

- metric trees
- experiment plans
- funnel dashboards
- cohort readouts
- causal caveats
- performance reviews

### Layer 6: Referral And Network Effects

Owns referral systems, partnerships, affiliate systems, flywheels, and network amplification.

Outputs:

- referral loops
- partner maps
- affiliate rules
- invite moment design
- loop economics
- fraud controls

## Canonical Router

Use `scripts/growth_skill_router.py` to classify requests into one or more layers, agent roles, and support skills.

## Connector Policy

CRM, email, SMS, analytics, scraping, SEO, ad, CMS, and social automation skills are execution modules. They do not own strategy. `growth-operating-system` owns strategy and measurement.
