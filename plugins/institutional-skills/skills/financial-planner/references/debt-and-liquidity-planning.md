# Debt and Liquidity Planning

## Debt Strategy

- inventory rates, terms, tax treatment, and covenants
- prioritize high-cost and variable-rate debt risk
- choose payoff/refinance/maintain decisions by objective

## Liquidity Strategy

- tier-1 operating cash
- tier-2 emergency reserve
- tier-3 tactical opportunity liquidity

## Metrics

- debt service ratio
- interest burden as % income
- months of liquidity coverage
