# Cash Flow and Balance Sheet Planning

## Process

- build monthly cash-flow baseline (recurring + irregular)
- separate fixed vs variable expenses
- quantify discretionary spend capacity
- map net-worth composition and concentration

## Targets

- sustainable savings rate aligned to goals
- emergency liquidity buffer aligned to income stability
- manageable fixed-obligation profile

## Monitoring

- savings rate trend
- spending variance vs plan
- liquidity runway months
