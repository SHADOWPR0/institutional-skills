# Behavioral Coaching and Governance

## Behavioral Risks

- panic selling and recency bias
- overconfidence and concentration creep
- goal drift and implementation procrastination

## Controls

- pre-commitment rules for rebalancing and de-risking
- checklist before major allocation changes
- quarterly accountability review with action closure tracking

## Communication Pattern

- state what changed, why, and what action follows
- separate market noise from plan-critical signals
