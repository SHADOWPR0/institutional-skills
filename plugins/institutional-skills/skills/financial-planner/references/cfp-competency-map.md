# CFP-Style Competency Map

This map captures broad planning competencies for comprehensive client planning.

## Core Domains

- professional conduct and fiduciary-style client care
- client discovery and data gathering
- risk management and insurance planning
- investment planning and policy alignment
- tax-aware planning coordination
- retirement accumulation and distribution planning
- estate and beneficiary coordination
- integrated plan presentation and monitoring

## Capability Outcomes

- convert client goals into measurable planning targets
- identify planning gaps and sequence actions by impact
- build coordinated recommendations across planning domains
- maintain a recurring review and update discipline
