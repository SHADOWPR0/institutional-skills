# Estate and Beneficiary Planning

## Coordination Topics

- beneficiary designations and titling alignment
- trust/will coordination checkpoints
- incapacity and directive document status
- liquidity for transfer costs and obligations

## Workflow

- map current structure and beneficiaries
- identify conflicts between account designations and intent
- prioritize corrections and periodic review cadence

## Boundary

Legal document drafting and interpretation must be completed by licensed counsel.
