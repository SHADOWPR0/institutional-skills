# Planning Formulas and Ratios

## Core Ratios

- Savings Rate = Annual Savings / Gross Income
- Debt Service Ratio = Annual Debt Payments / Gross Income
- Liquidity Coverage (months) = Liquid Assets / Monthly Core Expenses
- Net Worth Growth Rate = (Net Worth_t - Net Worth_t-1) / Net Worth_t-1
- Withdrawal Rate = Annual Portfolio Draw / Portfolio Value

## Use Rules

- treat ratios as diagnostics, not absolute pass/fail rules
- evaluate trend direction and context, not one-point snapshots
- recalculate after major life or market changes
