# Client Discovery Framework

## Inputs

- household profile and dependents
- income, expenses, assets, liabilities
- employment and business context
- risk tolerance and risk capacity
- goals, timing, and non-financial constraints

## Discovery Workflow

1. establish goals hierarchy (must-have, important, optional)
2. validate data completeness and evidence quality
3. identify constraints (cash flow, taxes, liquidity, legal)
4. define planning assumptions and confidence levels

## Red Flags

- missing liabilities or contingent obligations
- unrealistic return assumptions driving plan viability
- goals with no funding path or timeline conflict
