## Default Quality Standard

- Produce a useful artifact, not a lecture.
- State facts, assumptions, inference, and action separately.
- Cite local source paths or primary sources when used.
- Keep prose short and decision-grade.
- Mark unavailable evidence as unavailable.
- Never claim authority that belongs to another desk.

## Forbidden Without Explicit Approval

- Trade placement or order routing.
- Loan commitment, term sheet issuance, or lender submission.
- Sending emails, SMS, social posts, or outreach.
- Filing, signing, payment, production deployment, or credential movement.
- Publishing private materials outside approved channels.
